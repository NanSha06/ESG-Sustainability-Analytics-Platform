# ESG & Sustainability Analytics Platform

## Overview

The ESG & Sustainability Analytics Platform is an AI-powered business intelligence solution designed to help organizations monitor, analyze, report, and improve Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) performance.

The platform centralizes ESG-related data, automates reporting, generates sustainability insights, and helps organizations comply with regulatory frameworks.

---

# Business Problem

Organizations face difficulties with:

- Manual ESG reporting
- Fragmented sustainability data
- Regulatory compliance
- Carbon emission tracking
- ESG score calculation
- Sustainability planning

Most ESG reporting processes are time-consuming and heavily dependent on manual analysis.

---

# Proposed Solution

Build an AI-powered ESG Analytics Platform that:

- Aggregates ESG data
- Tracks sustainability metrics
- Generates ESG scores
- Produces automated reports
- Provides AI-powered recommendations

---

# Objectives

1. Centralize ESG-related information.
2. Automate sustainability reporting.
3. Monitor ESG KPIs.
4. Improve compliance and transparency.
